## Ablations (Step 7) in one line each
- **Shield vs no shield:** without the reserve the learned policy loses 147-173 medical device-h per outage; with it, 0.
- **Health twin vs headcount:** -11.1 % vulnerability-weighted heat in the shield's cooling order; -0.1 % (p = 3e-4) when used only in the learned policy's reward, because the shield's thermal floor already applies it.
- **Forecaster:** sizing the reserve from the forecast P90 (6-8 h, floored at 12 h) instead of 48 h lowers heat by 1.6 % with a warning (p = 1e-19), medical service unchanged; also using the forecast median as the restoration estimate makes fixed pacing empty the battery early and raises heat by 4.9 %.
- **24 h warning:** -13 % vulnerability-weighted heat for the shield, -15 % for the learned policy.

## Limitations to carry into the paper
- Simulation only; residents and health-twin weights are synthetic and illustrative.
- MAPPO trained for 320 outages per seed and stayed near its heat limit; longer training may change the trade-off.
- EAGLE-I county events measure time until most customers are restored, not a single home's outage; ordinary
  outages are much shorter than the multi-day heatwave outages the simulations test.
- CityLearn islanding uses the controller-layer workaround in `citylearn_port/run_citylearn.py`.
